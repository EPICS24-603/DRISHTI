## Project Vision

DRISHTI aims to provide an AI-powered assistive solution that can help visually impaired users understand their surroundings, interact through voice, and access information using their smartphone camera.

The project will continue to evolve through development, testing, optimization, and user-focused accessibility improvements until the final project phase in February 2027.
